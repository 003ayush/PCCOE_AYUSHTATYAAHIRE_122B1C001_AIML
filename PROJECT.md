Case Study 3 Automotive Cybersecurity TARA Assistant Project Overview Assist cybersecurity engineers with architecture-aware asset identification, attack-surface analysis, threat scenarios, mitigations, and traceability. 1. Business Context Threat Analysis and Risk Assessment under ISO/SAE 21434 requires engineers to interpret architecture, communication interfaces, data flows, assets, trust boundaries, and operational contexts. Manual execution is expertise-intensive, repeated across similar systems, and vulnerable to inconsistent threat coverage and mitigation selection. 2. Project Objective • Improve asset and attack-surface coverage. • Accelerate threat scenario generation and risk analysis. • Standardize mitigation recommendations. • Create a reusable, controlled cybersecurity knowledge base. 3.
